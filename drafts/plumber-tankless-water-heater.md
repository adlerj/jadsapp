# Plumber + Tankless Water Heater (draft)

**Planned category:** `life`
**Status:** Draft. Awaiting Q&A before drafting prose.

## Working angle

How to avoid getting rolled by contractors when you're the non-expert. Concrete story: contractors quoted absurd full-package prices for a tankless water heater install; sourced the unit online; hired a local plumber for ~$1,000 labor. Final cost a fraction of the quotes.

The post lives next to the tech-blog voice because it's senior-engineer systems thinking applied to home repair: decompose the bundled offering, source the parts separately, hire the labor commodity-style.

## What Jeff already shared

- Tried to find a plumber. All of them tried to rip him off (high quotes).
- Found a tankless water heater online.
- Hired a local plumber for $1,000 (labor only).
